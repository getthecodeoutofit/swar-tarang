About Swar Tarang

The heartbeat of musical excellence at Graphic Era Hill University, Bhimtal

Swar Tarang, established in 2024, is the premier music club of Graphic Era Hill University, Bhimtal. We are a passionate community of musicians and music enthusiasts driven by the power of melody to unite and inspire.

Heading into 2026, as the reigning best band in the university with over 20 trophies, we continue to set new benchmarks in musical performance. Our club offers a supportive platform for every student to shine and grow.
